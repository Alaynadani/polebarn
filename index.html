Country Hall Tradition

First Time on the Hardwood? You're Right at Home.

Line dancing has endured for over a century because it belongs to everyone. No judgment, no expensive equipment—just friendly people, good songs, and rhythm under your soles.

directions_walk Center Floor Strategy

Stand in the middle row so you always have someone in front of you to watch when we rotate walls!

roller_skating Smooth-Soled Footwear

Leather-soled boots or flat street shoes work best so your knees turn freely without sticking to the maple boards.
