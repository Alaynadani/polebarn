The Electric Slide

The granddaddy of line dances. Grapevine right, grapevine left, step touches, and an energetic brush-kick with quarter turn.

Suggested Track: "Electric Boogie" / Marcia Griffiths or any 105 BPM shuffle
Key Counts: Counts 1-4 Vine right, 5-8 Vine left, 9-12 Step back touches, 13-18 Hop quarter turn.
Taught: Tuesday
stairs Beginner Friendly 16 Count • 4 Wall

Cowboy Charleston

Quick, rhythmic toe taps forward and backward with a smooth jazz-age swing adapted to country western dance floors.

Suggested Track: "Achy Breaky Heart" / Billy Ray Cyrus or "Fishin' in the Dark"
Key Counts: Right point forward, step back, left touch back, step forward, quarter pivot turn.
Taught: Tuesday
stairs Beginner Friendly 40 Count • 4 Wall

Watermelon Crawl

Iconic country novelty dance with heel splits, side toe touches, and the legendary crawl knee-rub rhythm in the chorus.

Suggested Track: "Watermelon Crawl" / Tracy Byrd (134 BPM)
Key Counts: Toe-heel struts, grapevine with quarter turn, knee swivels ('the crawl').
Taught: Tue & Fri
stairs Beginner Friendly 32 Count • 4 Wall

Cupid Shuffle (Country Style)

Step side-to-side, heel kicks, and 8-count marching quarter pivot. Danced with southern boots and country swagger.

Suggested Track: "Cupid Shuffle" or Country remix by Colt Ford
Key Counts: Side touches right (1-4), side touches left (5-8), heel digs (9-16), walk turns (17-24).
Taught: Tuesday
bolt Improver Stomp 24 Count • 4 Wall

Copperhead Road

The heart-pounding country rock anthem. Aggressive boot heel digs, syncopated scuffs, and a thunderous barn-wide jump-turn.

Suggested Track: "Copperhead Road" / Steve Earle (76/152 BPM)
Key Counts: Heel tap, hook, heel tap, step. Repeat left. Jump half turn and double stomp.
Taught: Thu & Fri
bolt Improver Stomp 40 Count • 4 Wall

The Tush Push

Choreographed by Jim Ferrazzano. Double heel touches, cha-cha rock steps forward and back, and rhythmic pelvic bumps.

Suggested Track: "Chattahoochee" / Alan Jackson or "Third Rock from the Sun"
Key Counts: Double heel digs, coaster steps, half turn pivots, and 4 hip pushes.
Taught: Thursday
bolt Improver Stomp 32 Count • 4 Wall

Boot Scootin' Boogie

Bill Bader's legendary 1991 choreography that fueled the 90s country explosion. Smooth heel swivels and hitch steps.

Suggested Track: "Boot Scootin' Boogie" / Brooks & Dunn (130 BPM)
Key Counts: Heel swivels right, vine right, heel swivels left, vine left, scoot steps.
Taught: Thu & Fri

Shivers

Choreographed by Alison Johnstone. Modern syncopated side cross steps, coaster back points, and upbeat pop energy.

Suggested Track: "Shivers" / Ed Sheeran (141 BPM)
Key Counts: Side point cross touches, forward coaster step, shuffle back, rolling sailor turn.
Taught: Friday
music_note Country Pop 48 Count • 4 Wall

Good Time

Choreographed by Jenny Cain. Joyful strut routine with heel touches, rock recovers, and snappy finger snaps on the chorus.

Suggested Track: "Good Time" / Alan Jackson (132 BPM)
Key Counts: Heel heel forward, side behind side cross, sailor steps, snap turn.
Taught: Thursday
hourglass_top Waltz Rhythm 48 Count • 2 Wall

The Tennessee Waltz

Graceful 3/4 box step line dance. Forward balance steps, twinkling crosses, and soft gliding turns that give tired boots a graceful rest.

Suggested Track: "Tennessee Waltz" / Iva Zanicchi or Patti Page (88 BPM 3/4)
Key Counts: Waltz forward (1-2-3), waltz back (4-5-6), twinkle left, twinkle right.
Taught: Monthly
